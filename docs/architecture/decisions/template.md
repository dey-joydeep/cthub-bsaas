# ADR-NNNN: Short Decision Title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Rejected | Superseded | Recorded baseline
- **Date recorded:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Original decision date:** YYYY-MM-DD or unknown for a retrospective record
- **Owner:** Role or named decision owner
- **Decision maker:** Role or person; pending while proposed
- **Related issue/PR:** Links
- **Supersedes / superseded by:** Links, if applicable

## Context

Describe the problem, constraints, current behavior, and why a decision is needed now. Link the relevant requirements, HLD/LLD, contracts, code, and operational evidence.

## Decision drivers

- State the criteria that matter and how they will be evaluated.

## Decision

While proposed, state **Pending** and the evidence or owner choice still needed. When accepted, state the chosen option and why it best meets the drivers. Do not turn a proposal into a decision by implication.

For a retrospective `Recorded baseline`, describe the choice evidenced by current docs or implementation. Separate documented rationale from inference and state which historical alternatives or approvals are unknown.

## Consequences and follow-up

Describe implementation and migration effects, compatibility, security, operations, testing, rollback, and known tradeoffs. Link follow-up work rather than treating this ADR as authorization to execute it.

## Review and history

Record the independent reviewer, reviewed commit or PR, findings and disposition when required. Add dated status changes here. When superseded, link the replacement ADR and retain this record for history.
